## FEMA PA

FEMA Public Assistance funds state, local, and certain nonprofit applicants after a declared disaster. Debris, emergency work, public facilities. Project-based. Obligation is not cash in the door. Insurance can reduce what FEMA pays.

## Five STAR cues

- **Pronghorn:** reimbursements, policy exceptions, 75% → 98% (**confirm the number**)  
- **BDO:** journal entries, bank confirmations, trace the mismatch, workpapers  
- **E Fund:** 70k+ rows, Python/SQL, validation, exception list  
- **Alibaba:** settlements, refunds/chargebacks/delays, Finance + Product review  
- **Columbia ERM:** COSO / ISO 31000 → practical recommendations, not a quote sheet
